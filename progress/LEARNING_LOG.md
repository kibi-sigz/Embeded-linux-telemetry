# Learning journal

[← Tracker](TRACKER.md) | [← Dashboard](../README.md)

Add a new dated entry after each study session. Keep it short and honest: what you did, what failed, and the exact next action.

## Session template

Copy this block for each session:

```text
Date:
Phase:
Time spent:
What I learned:
Commands/code I ran:
What worked:
What failed / what I don't understand:
Next action:
```
